How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.

Green means the letter is in the correct position.

Yellow means the letter is in the word but in a different position.

Grey means the letter is not in the word.

Type with your keyboard or use the on-screen keys. Press Enter to submit a guess and Backspace to delete a letter. You can start another puzzle after each game.
